ABOUT THIS JOURNAL

Curiosity, with
receipts.

Making Sense of Complex Problems is an independent publication about the systems, decisions and ideas that shape everyday life.

It follows questions across industries and disciplines—through research, history, observation and the occasional detour. The aim isn't to arrive at a clever answer quickly. It's to understand the problem well enough to ask a better question.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Written by a product and technology practitioner interested in how people, organisations and complex systems interact. The author's perspective is informed by experience across software quality, product work and industrial technology—but the investigations are driven by curiosity, not a résumé.
